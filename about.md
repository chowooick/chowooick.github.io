I am building **Mars**, a session-based economic social sim: you win a lottery
ticket to Mars, you produce and trade to stay alive, and when you die you stay
on the map as a gravestone. Three to five minutes per session. No gacha, no
combat, no PvP. Server-authoritative, because an economy that can be edited
client-side is not an economy.

Target: mobile early access 2027-01-14, Steam 2027-03-02. Solo developer,
zero marketing budget.

The work is done through AI coding agents running one ticket each, in parallel,
against a single repository. This blog is where the findings go — the ones that
cost hours to discover and take one paragraph to explain.

Stack: Godot 4.7.2, Nakama 3.40 (self-hosted, Go runtime), PostgreSQL.
